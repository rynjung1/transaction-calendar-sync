Privacy Policy

What this app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This policy is written to meet Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), since the app is built for use with a Canadian bank account.

Who this policy covers

Transaction Calendar Sync is operated by Ryan Jung. There is no separate corporate entity at this time — this policy will be updated if that changes.

The app does not collect your bank login credentials — those go directly to Plaid and your bank. The app only ever receives a secure access token from Plaid, encrypted before it's stored.

The app does not read your existing calendar events. It only requests permission to list your calendars, so you can pick one, and to add new events to the one you choose.

How your information is used

Solely to provide the app's one function: turning your bank transactions into calendar events on the calendar you pick, and showing you a spending summary inside the app. Your data is never sold, and never used for advertising.

Who else touches your information

  • Plaid — connects to your bank and provides transaction data. See Plaid's privacy policy.
  • Supabase — hosts the app's database and handles sign-in. Data is stored in the United States (AWS, us-east-1).
  • Vercel — runs the app's backend logic, also hosted in the United States.
  • Apple / Google — if you choose an iCloud or Google calendar, events the app creates live there under that provider's own terms, not this app's.
  • Sentry — if enabled, receives crash and error reports (device/OS information, a stack trace, and diagnostic details about what the app was doing when something went wrong) so problems can be found and fixed. Your bank login, your Plaid access token, and your app password are never sent to Sentry or anywhere else outside the app's own backend. See Sentry's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare — if enabled, its Turnstile service runs an automated check when you sign in or sign up, to help prevent automated abuse of the app's login. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
Cross-border disclosure

Because Supabase and Vercel host their infrastructure in the United States, your personal information is processed and stored outside Canada, and may be accessible to foreign courts, law enforcement, or national security authorities under that jurisdiction's laws.

Security

Your Plaid access token is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before it's stored — never held in plain text. Every request to the app's backend is authenticated and scoped to your own account, so it only ever reads or writes your own data; the database itself is also locked down so it can't be queried directly from outside the app's own backend. Your session is stored in your device's secure hardware-backed storage (iOS Keychain), not plain app storage.

No security measure is perfect. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm to you, you'll be notified as soon as feasible, consistent with PIPEDA's breach notification requirements.

How long your information is kept

As long as your account is active. There's currently no automatic deletion after a period of inactivity — you can request or perform deletion at any time.

Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • See your own data — the app shows every synced transaction, in Home and Insights.
  • Correct or update it — sync preferences are editable in Settings. Transaction data itself comes directly from your bank via Plaid and reflects what actually happened in your account, so it isn't hand-editable.
  • Ask a question or file a complaint — email ryanjung2007@gmail.com. If unresolved, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
Delete your account

In-app: Settings → Delete account. This permanently revokes your bank connection with Plaid, erases every synced transaction, and removes your account — this can't be undone. It does not delete calendar events already created; those live in your own calendar and are yours to manage.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a change affects how your information is used, you'll be notified in-app or by email before it takes effect.
