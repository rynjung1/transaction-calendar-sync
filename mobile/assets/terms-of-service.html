What the app does

The app connects to your bank account through Plaid, reads your transaction data, and writes each transaction as an event on a calendar you choose. It's a read-only spending diary — the app never initiates a payment, transfer, or any change to your bank account, and never manages your money in any way. See the Privacy Policy for what data is collected and how it's used.
